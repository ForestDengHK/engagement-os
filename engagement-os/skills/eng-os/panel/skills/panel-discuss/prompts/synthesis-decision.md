## Synthesis: decision

Produce output in this order:

### 📋 Options
Enumerate the options considered. One line each.

### ⚖ Pros/Cons matrix
Table or per-role blocks. Each cell concrete, citing `[E-id]`s where relevant.

### 🗳 Preferences
List each role's preferred option with their rationale.

### 📌 Recommendation
The recommended option, grounded in the strongest pros cited and the fewest
red-line conflicts. If roles disagreed, say so and explain the recommendation's
basis.

### ❓ Open Questions
Things that must be resolved before the decision is actionable.

### 📎 Action Items
Concrete next steps with owners.
