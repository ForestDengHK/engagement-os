## Synthesis: alignment

Produce output in this order:

### ✅ Alignment points
Where roles agreed — specific, not generic.

### ⚠ Misalignment points
Where roles disagreed or diverged in understanding. For each, name the roles
involved and the nature of the disagreement.

### ❓ Clarifications needed
Questions the panel couldn't resolve internally — must be asked to the client,
another stakeholder, or settled by a specific document.
