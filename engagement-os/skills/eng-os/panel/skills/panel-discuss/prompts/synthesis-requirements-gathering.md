## Synthesis: requirements-gathering

Produce output in this order:

### ⚙ Functional
Consolidated list, deduplicated. Attribute which role(s) raised each.

### 📐 Non-functional
Same structure, grouped by dimension (performance / availability / security / etc.).

### 🔒 Constraints
External constraints the requirements must satisfy.

### ✅ Acceptance criteria (by requirement)
Keyed to each requirement above. Each criterion testable — names a specific
condition, threshold, or artifact.

### 📬 Open items
Requirements the panel couldn't fully specify. For each, what's missing and
who can provide it.
