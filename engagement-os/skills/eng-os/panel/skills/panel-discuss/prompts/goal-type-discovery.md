## Goal-type instructions: discovery

The goal is to map what we know, don't know, and assume — not to decide. Each
contribution classifies claims into three buckets.

Your contribution should include:
- **Known** — facts you have evidence for, with `[E-id]` citations
- **Assumed** — beliefs you are operating on but have not verified
- **Unknown** — things you wish you knew to give better advice
- 1–3 specific questions to ask next (to the client, to another role, to a document)
