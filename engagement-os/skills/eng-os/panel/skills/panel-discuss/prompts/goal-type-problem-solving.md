## Goal-type instructions: problem-solving

Frame the discussion as: what is the problem, what could cause it, what
resolves it. Each contribution should bring candidate root causes from your
specialty and concrete investigation steps.

Your contribution should include:
- Problem statement from your angle (what does this look like from your role?)
- 1–3 candidate root causes with evidence pointers (cite `[E-id]`s)
- For your top root cause: the next investigation step (a specific action, not "look into it")
