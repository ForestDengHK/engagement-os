## Synthesis: brainstorm

Produce output in this order:

### 🌱 Ideas (grouped)
Cluster ideas from all contributions. Label each cluster with a short theme.
Attribute ideas to the role that raised them.

### 🌪 Wild options
Ideas named as "wild" by their authors, plus any that read as outside mainstream
consulting recommendations. Don't judge — list.

### 🌟 Promising candidates
3–5 ideas that drew positive engagement from multiple roles. Mark which roles
endorsed each.

Do NOT recommend a winner. The point of brainstorm is to leave options open.
