## Goal-type instructions: decision

For each option on the table, give explicit pros and cons from your role's
angle. Mark your own preferred option clearly. Do not suppress minority views —
if only you hold a position, that is the signal the panel needs.

Your contribution should include:
- Pros/Cons per option from your role's priorities
- Your preferred option with rationale
- Any red-line violations in specific options (this is a hard rejection, not a con)
- One concrete next-step if your preferred option is selected
