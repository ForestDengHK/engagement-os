## Synthesis: discovery

Produce output in this order:

### 📚 Known
Consolidated Known items across all roles, deduplicated. Cite `[E-id]`s.

### 🧭 Unknown
Consolidated Unknown items. Cluster related unknowns.

### ❓ Assumptions
What the panel is currently assuming. Flag any assumption that multiple roles
would treat as critical to validate before acting.

### 🔎 Questions to ask next
Concrete questions for named audiences (client stakeholders, specific documents,
other roles not in the panel).

### 🕳 Evidence gaps
Specific documents or data the panel wishes it had. Actionable — each should
be something a human can go retrieve.
