## Goal-type instructions: brainstorm

Go wider than your usual comfort zone. Cross-pollinate freely — if an earlier
speaker's idea has an adjacent territory you'd stretch into, name it. Actively
resist converging to a single answer in this round; breadth is the point.

Your contribution should include:
- 2–4 specific ideas from your role's angle (not generic)
- At least one "wild option" — something you'd normally reject but name for completeness
- At least one cross-pollination ("taking X's point and pushing it further...")

Do NOT recommend a winner. Do NOT rank. Do NOT narrow.
