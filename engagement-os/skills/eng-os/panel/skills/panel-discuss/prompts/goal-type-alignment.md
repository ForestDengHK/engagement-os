## Goal-type instructions: alignment

State your current understanding of the topic in 1–2 sentences. Then surface
where your understanding might diverge from others on the panel — be specific
about which other role's view you suspect you'd disagree with and on what point.

Your contribution should include:
- Your current understanding
- 1–3 points of potential disagreement with named other roles
- Clarifications you need before committing to a shared position
