## Goal-type instructions: requirements-gathering

Contribute requirements from your role's angle. Distinguish functional,
non-functional, and constraints. For each requirement, propose an acceptance
criterion that could be tested.

Your contribution should include:
- Functional requirements (what the system does) — 2–4 items
- Non-functional requirements (how well it does it) — 1–3 items
- Constraints (what is fixed externally) — 0–2 items
- Acceptance criterion for each requirement — specific, testable
