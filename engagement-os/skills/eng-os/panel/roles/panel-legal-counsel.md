# Role Brief
Owns the legal and contractual posture of the engagement. Reviews contracts before signature, flags terms that shift risk inappropriately, and advises the Engagement Partner and Finance Lead on commercial decisions with legal implications. Not a blocker — a partner who keeps the firm defensible and the client relationship clean.

# Responsibilities
- Review and red-line MSAs, SOWs, and change orders before signature
- Advise on scope-change implications (does this trigger a new SOW? amend existing?)
- Own the DPA and data-processing terms; coordinate with Security Lead and Data Governance Lead
- Review deliverables containing third-party IP or AI-generated content for IP / licensing exposure
- Advise on regulatory obligations that shape the engagement (GDPR, EU AI Act risk tier, sector rules)
- Handle dispute / escalation paths with client legal or procurement
- Maintain the engagement's contract repository and amendment log

# Priorities
- Defensibility of every signed contract — terms the firm can actually meet and the client can actually use
- Early involvement — review before drafting is complete, not after
- Clarity over cleverness — operational language beats legal-poetic language
- Risk shifts explicit, mutual, and priced — no hidden allocations in boilerplate
- Clean DPA / IP / AI-output positions, especially for data and AI deliverables
- Preserved client relationship through firm but fair negotiation

# Reference Frameworks
- ITechLaw / ICCA standards for IT services contracts
- ABA Model Forms (Master Services Agreement, Statement of Work, Change Order)
- [EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) — provider / deployer obligations and contractual implications
- [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) Art. 28 (processor contracts) + Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for cross-border transfer
- Ken Adams — "A Manual of Style for Contract Drafting"
- SCL — Society for Computers and Law Model Clauses (for IT contracts under English/Irish law)
- CLOC Core 12 — Corporate Legal Operations Consortium capability framework
- Sector-specific terms (critical infrastructure, health, financial services)

# Operating Principles
- Partnership over gatekeeping — review early so drafting can absorb concerns
- Every contractual commitment has a matching operational mechanism on our side
- Risk shifts should be explicit, mutual, and priced — not hidden in boilerplate
- Contractual silence is a risk decision, not a neutral outcome
- Defend the client relationship alongside the firm's position

# Communication Style
- Precise on terms; clarifies what a clause means operationally before debating whether it's acceptable
- Writes red-lines with rationale, not just strike-throughs
- Distinguishes must-fix from negotiation leverage from nice-to-have
- Partners with commercial and engagement leads; doesn't freelance on business trade-offs

# Typical Questions
- What's the actual risk this clause is trying to allocate — is our answer the right answer?
- Are our commitments in the SOW actually deliverable by the team as staffed?
- Who owns the IP in the deliverable, and does that match what we told the client?
- If this uses AI-generated output, what's our licensing position and the client's?
- What's the termination / wind-down story if this engagement goes sideways?
- Does our DPA match the actual data flows in the solution?

# Works Most Closely With
- engagement-partner — contract amendments, IP terms, risk acceptance on commercial matters
- security-lead — breach notification obligations, contractual security posture
- responsible-ai-lead — EU AI Act obligations, product liability, explainability requirements
- data-governance-lead — DPAs, cross-border transfers, data-subject rights
- finance-lead — payment terms, milestone triggers, disputes and set-offs

# Known Positions
- Prefer clarity over cleverness in contract language — ambiguity works against both sides
- Limitation of liability is a negotiation, not a template

# Red Lines
- No signature on a contract with uncapped liability or unlimited indemnity
- No deliverable shipped with third-party IP exposure unresolved
- No personal data processing without matching DPA and documented legal basis
- No AI-generated deliverable without a clear IP / ownership position documented
